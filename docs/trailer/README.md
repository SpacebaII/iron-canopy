# First Strike: the teaser

`iron-canopy-first-strike.mp4` is a proof-of-concept teaser (brief 60), 74 seconds: a missile raid on the capital's airport at dusk, told in order on the map and in the 3D view.

Every picture in it is the game itself. Nothing is drawn by hand or edited after: the raid is played live in the Sandbox, the map's shots are filmed as it happens, and the 3D shots replay the game's own record of it. The music and sound effects are generated by a script, timed to the cut and to the explosions in each shot.

## What happens

1. Holitz, the capital, at 19:36. Its airport, the country's busiest, in the last light.
2. Across the border, a ballistic missile leaves its launcher. Six of them arc across the map.
3. Sixteen cruise missiles come in low over the fields. One passes 45 m from the camera.
4. The defence wakes. A medium-range battery fires, a cruise missile is shot down, and a ballistic missile is met 20 km up.
5. Not all of them are stopped. Two cruise missiles reach the airport, and it burns.
6. The camera pulls back to the whole map, then the title.

The raid is not scripted. The defence was simply given too few missiles. The cut picks its shots from what happened in that run: which battery fired first, which missile was shot down, and where the ones that got through landed.

## How it was made

The film is made by tools in the repository:

- **Film mode** (brief 58, `film.js`, `film-cam.js`) hides the interface and adds letterbox bars, a film grade and captions. It runs the game on a held clock, so every frame is drawn in full however slow the machine is.
- **3D film cameras and war effects** (brief 59, `render3d-war.js`):
  - cameras planted beside a flight path, long lenses, chase shots and orbits;
  - smoke trails, fireballs, smoke columns, launch clouds and burning debris.
- **The teaser script** (`tools/teaser.js`) stages the raid, films each shot, and cuts the clips together with ffmpeg.
- **The score** (`tools/teaser-score.js`) writes the music and sound effects.

This copy was filmed at 1280 × 720 and 24 frames a second, with the 3D view at Medium quality. The machine had no graphics card, so each 3D frame took about 4 seconds and the whole film about 1½ hours.

To make it again (it needs Playwright, three.js on disk and ffmpeg):

```
npm i --no-save playwright three@0.160.0
node tools/teaser.js --q=medium --w=1280 --h=720 --fps=24
```

The default settings are 1920 × 1080, 30 fps and High quality, for a machine with a graphics card. Useful options:

- `--stills` saves one picture from each shot, for checking the cut quickly.
- `--only=calm,hit` films just those shots.
- `--cut` cuts and scores the clips already filmed.

# Second Cut

`iron-canopy-second-cut.mp4` (89 seconds, 12 MB, plays on a phone) is the same raid filmed again after briefs 61–64:

- **Models with moving parts:** the enemy's launchers, our batteries and radars. A launcher raises its missile, a battery swings round to its target, and each round leaves from its own tube.
- **Effects at the source:**
  - a flame on the nozzle;
  - a launch cloud and dust at the launcher;
  - fireballs strung along the path of the missile that was hit, with pieces that fall;
  - ground hits with a shock ring, debris, scorch and a smoke column.
- **Sound designed per shot:** the score.

It was made with `node tools/teaser.js --q=medium --w=1280 --h=720 --fps=24 --out=docs/trailer/iron-canopy-second-cut.mp4`, about 1¾ hours on a machine with no graphics card. It was then compressed for phones: H.264 at CRF 23, AAC sound, with `+faststart`.
