# 78 Counter-strikes: talk it over with Gen. Voss, then watch the jets go

Owns `js/strike.js` (headless), `js/ui-strike.js` and `css/strike.css` (the Air room's Counter-strike page), and the `xstrike` mission in `air.js`.

## What the owner said

> "attacks on enemy weapons … should be in a menu and dialogue that he'll negotiate with a military chief that will conduct strikes and he'll be able to pick a certain sector and some sort of launcher and knock it out before next wave and it'll have rng … see the planes leave the airport and then they'll go over there either get shot or destroy x numbers of targets and those won't launch in the next attack"

## What this brief does

- **The compass**: their batteries by sector from our capital, with how many launchers are known to be there and how many we have located (a launch we track gives its battery away; a launcher found by the usual sensors is exact).
- **What to hit**: ballistic launchers, cruise missile launchers, drone sites, rocket artillery, aircraft at their bases, their air defence. One strike goes for the battery we know most about and any of its kind within 60 km.
- **Voss's advice** in her own words: their air defence over the target, their fighters, what we know, each jet's chance of being lost, and three packages (two jets; four and an escort; six, an escort and radar hunters) with the aircraft lost and launchers destroyed to expect. She recommends one and will not fly a package that costs too many crews for what it gets: how far she goes is her trust in you. The player may order it anyway; it costs her trust.
- **The jets fly**: the flights take off from their bases, cross the border, and over the target the dice decide, jet by jet: shot down, or a launcher destroyed (70% if located, 25% if hunted). A report card and a line from Voss follow. What dies does not fire in the next wave; they answer with the plan's retaliation wave. One strike between raids (two at a trust of 70).

## Odds (`IC.STRIKE`)

Per jet: 3% plus 6% for each of their air defence launchers covering the target (halved by radar hunters) plus 2.5% for each of their fighters within 250 km (halved by an escort), at most 60%. A strike reaches 42% of a fighter's endurance at cruise.
