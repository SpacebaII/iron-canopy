# 66 A road hierarchy, interchanges by their setting, and traffic that only has to look alive

On its own branch, stacked on brief 65. Owns the ambient traffic (`assign` in `traffic.js`), the car parks' day (`IC.PARK_DAY`, `IC.landUse` in `landside.js`), the collectors (`IC.FABRIC.col`, class `col`), the markings at the avenues' crossings (`IC.paintStreetJoin` in `render-roads.js`), and the new interchange shapes (`junctions` in `gen.js`: `dirT`, `stack`, `turbine`, `spui`, `parclo`).

## What this brief does

1. **The lights are not drawn.** The junctions with lights keep them (`IC.signalFor`): vehicles still stop, queue and go by turn, but no heads stand at the kerb.
2. **Traffic only has to look alive** (`assign`). No trips are planned any more: no Dijkstra from every zone, no trips between every pair of places. Every link carries a flow from what is round it, by its class (`GATHER`): in town the homes, jobs and freight of the zones within 2.5 km, more towards the middle of town, with the commuters' share running towards the jobs in the morning and home in the evening (and into the offices and out of the homes on the streets where each zone's trips start and end); on the national roads the people of every town, near ones counting for most and the far term carrying the traffic between towns 170–400 km apart; freight near the depots, plants and crossings. A street or road keeps one character along its length (a hash of the street, not of each piece), so the traffic does not change from block to block. The loads are scaled class by class as before, a town's at the 99.5th percentile and the roads' at the 97th, and the national roads keep a floor too (`IC.TRAFFIC_LOAD.floor`, `gammaRoad`). Rebuilding the traffic takes about half the time it did.
3. **The airport is the one place traffic goes to.** Each of our airports is a place on the road network again (its middle is the runways: its road is the nearest that is not a motorway, up to 4 km off), and its travellers, taxis and shuttles run on the roads to the three nearest towns within 150 km, as many as it carries. Its car parks fill over the morning and empty in the evening (`IC.PARK_DAY`): the long-stay cars stay the night, so they never empty.
4. **Collectors** (`col`, 14 m: a lane each way either side of a dashed centre line, trees in the verge). Traced between the arterials and the side streets, about every second block between two arterials (`IC.FABRIC.col` = 1.75), as long streets that run through, so a town reads arterial, collector, local street. The traffic gives them their own class.
5. **Junctions marked like real ones.** Where two avenues or an avenue and a collector cross, the inner lane turns left (a turn arrow) and a hatched median opens the centre line into it; a side street meeting a bigger one without a zebra gives way, with a row of white triangles across its lane.
6. **Interchanges by their setting.** A motorway ending on another by a big city (300,000 people, within 4 km of its edge) is a directional T: no loop, the two left turns on flyovers that cross each other over the stem, drawn over the roads below with their shadow and parapets. Four motorways crossing by a big city would be a stack or a turbine (rare on these maps). By a big town a main road's diamond is usually a single-point interchange, its four slip roads meeting at one junction on the bridge; out in the country about a quarter of the diamonds are partial cloverleaves, a loop and a slip road in the two quarters on one side, so nothing stands on the road's other side. The drive graph now reads which way a slip road runs over its first 400 m, not its first piece.
7. **Estates' closes fixed.** A close ended at its turning head only until the streets were tied together, which ran many of them on across the grass to the loop or the next close, with parked cars and traffic along lines nothing drew. A close now keeps its head.

## Worth knowing

- The flows are no longer routes: a vehicle close in still picks its next road by the loads, so busy roads are busy, but nothing says where a commuter is going. The bus and coach lines and the airport shuttles are still routed.
- The new shapes change the world's dice where they appear, so saves from before are refused, as with any change to world generation.
- Streets are drawn in the tiles, which are sharp to about 32 px a 100 m: the give-way triangles on side streets show on the avenues' crossings drawn live, and at the tiles' closest level they are a pixel or two.

## Done when

- `npm test` green, with tests that the roads are alive without trips (every national road carries something, more by the towns than far out; an avenue keeps its character along its length; the airport's roads carry its travellers; its car park fills by noon and empties at night but never to nothing), and that the interchanges are what their setting asks for (directional T junctions only by big cities, with two flyovers that cross; single-point interchanges' slip roads meeting the road at one point; partial cloverleaves folded to one side; every slip road of them one way).
