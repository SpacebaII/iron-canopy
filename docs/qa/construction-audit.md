# Construction audit (brief 47)

The owner: "there is still construction issues like ramps starting not on the same plane as the taxi way coming off at like a 45 degree angle … we need to make the fuel and fire feel more meaningful … a player can feel as if nothing is meaning anything … nothing is possible until all the prereqs are met and it never allows them to see a problem … construction ran into the double click issue again … we need a large QA audit on construction for airports."

Each finding: what was wrong, the picture, the cause, the fix and its commit (or why it stays), and the test that proves it. Pictures are in `docs/tasks/47-screens/` (made by `node tools/construction-qa.js`; the before pictures with `--root=` pointed at a copy of `wave11/airport-polish`, the branch this work started from). `qa-report.json` there is the machine log of the tool cycles.

## The QA pass

`node tools/construction-qa.js` runs every item of the build bar through the real page at the zoom it is used at (a picture of each placed plan with its Build button, `tool-<item>-placed.jpg`), and for runway, taxiway, apron, hangar, fuel farm, fire station and tower at the four zooms (`-placed-z6`, `-z30`, `-z110`, `-z300`: the whole airport, a terminal, a stand, the closest). For each it checks: the plan is placed, nothing is built on a click or by clicking the last point again, Esc takes it away, Build builds it, the work runs through its stages (paving seen for every one, `-stage.jpg` for the main items), it is in use when finished (its panel's line in `qa-report.json`), it can be bulldozed (salvage), and built again and undone (refund).

Every tool passed every step, with these notes:

- **Stand**: one click places a stand, or removes the stand clicked. A stand is ₭0.5M of paint, placed many at a time, and Ctrl+Z takes it back, so it stays a one-click tool (it never built on a second click).
- **Landing system**: refused at both ends of the international layout's runway, which already has one each (finding 15).
- **Blueprint**: the whole of a real airport could not all be bulldozed at once after six game hours: aircraft had parked on its aprons (the refusal says so). Not a fault.
- **Service road**: open at once (a strip of asphalt), so there is no stage to watch; it is undone, not bulldozed.
- Every tool's placing, cancelling, building, bulldozing and undoing is covered by the tests named above; the QA tool repeats them in the page with the interface.

Founding (`found-placed.jpg`): the site placed with its Found button and turning buttons, the runway turned by R, the survey in the help line.

The fuel farm, fire station and tower: `services-*.jpg` at the four zooms, `services-3d-tower.jpg`, `services-3d-farm.jpg`, `services-3d-fire.jpg` in the 3D view (the tower is seen from across the airport). The drill picture (`services-fire-drill`) timed out on the shared machine; the drill is covered by its test.

Before and after: `before-ramp.jpg` (the old builder: the apron askew to a taxiway at 30°, red, "covers a taxiway") against `ramp-flush.jpg`; `before-click-again.jpg` (the old "click again to build") against any `tool-*-placed.jpg`; `before-services.jpg` (the 26 m tank, the small shed) against `services-terminal.jpg`.
