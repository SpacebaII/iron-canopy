# Iron Canopy v1.0: playtester guide

Thank you for playing. This is one page: what to try, what we want to know, and how to tell us.

## Before you start

- Use a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox) on a screen at least 1280 × 720, full screen if you can.
- Everything saves in your browser. Esc opens the menu with "Save the game"; the game also saves itself every 10 game minutes and when you quit to the menu.
- Pause is Space. Esc always backs out of whatever is open.

## What to try (about an hour)

1. **The Career, the first 20 minutes, without reading anything first.** Press Enter on the start screen. Found the national airport, build its runway, an apron and a taxiway, and get the first airliner in. Tell us where you got stuck or were unsure what to do next.
2. **Build something bigger.** A second runway, a parallel taxiway, more stands. Does the builder snap where you expect? Did any cost, message or warning surprise you?
3. **Let time run.** Use the speeds (1–6), S and Wait (7). Sign airline deals in the Aviation room (V) and read the month's statement in the Economy room (E). Did the money make sense?
4. **Chapter 3, the airspace**, if you get there: put up radars, draw airways and shape the rings round your airport in its Airspace tab.
5. **A Quick war** from the start screen, for half an hour: raids, air defence and the 3D replay (Replay in the Journal, J).
6. **One Academy lesson** of your choice.

## What we want to know

- Anything that broke: an error, a frozen screen, a button that did nothing.
- Any text that was wrong, unclear, or showed "undefined", "NaN" or odd numbers.
- Anything that overlapped, was cut off or flickered.
- The moment you were bored, lost or annoyed, and the moment you had the most fun.
- Your screen size, browser, and whether it ran smoothly (it should hold 60 frames a second).

## How to send feedback

- A few lines are plenty. Put the **game date and time** from the top bar (for example "March, Year 1, 14:20") next to each point.
- A **screenshot** helps more than a description.
- If something broke, attach your **save**: Saved games (start screen or Settings) → Export, and send the file.
- Send it to the address or the thread you got this link from.
